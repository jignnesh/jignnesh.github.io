Rules built into this page

  • Never change plan or PFM because of one bad year or a market rally; judge PFMs on 3Y, 5Y and 10Y.
  • After a fall of 20% or more, do nothing for 30 days and write down your reasoning first.
  • Plan: LC75 High until Aggressive holds equal or more equity (age 42), then Aggressive to exit; check at 50, 55, 60 and 65.
  • PFM: act only if bottom half on both 5Y and 10Y and behind Nifty 50 TRI for two years, and a challenger passes every switch test (5Y +0.50 pp, 3Y +0.25 pp, 10Y not more than 0.25 pp lower, 5+ years of history, no red flag).
  • Limits: up to 4 plan changes and 1 PFM change per financial year (confirm in your NPS app).
