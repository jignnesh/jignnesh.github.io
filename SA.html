Reading the plot: the teal line goes flat the moment hill climbing has no improving move left — that's it, permanently stuck. The SA line keeps moving after that point, occasionally ticking upward (a deliberately accepted worse move) while it's still hot. At the end of its schedule it reports the best tour it ever visited, not just wherever the random walk happened to land.
Trap maps: the 10 preset maps (10–19 cities) were found by computational search and verified so that first-improvement hill climbing deterministically lands 10–24% above the optimal tour every single time, while simulated annealing's median run lands within 0–2% of optimal — a real, reproducible gap, not a lucky run.
